Behind the Canvas

I am a visual artist currently working out of my studio in Portland, Oregon. My work explores the intersection of organic textures, structural geometry, and the delicate balance of light within abstract digital frameworks.

With a background in fine arts and traditional oil painting, I transitioned into digital media to blur the lines between tactical depth and screen-based experiences. Every piece I create focuses on evoking an immediate spatial emotion through rich color interactions.
