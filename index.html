Rising of Venus

I believe that contemporary art should not forget the classical painting technique that old masters have developed for centuries, but we should also create something new and previously unknown through constant experimentation. My work has echoes of Botticelli's Venus and features of classical art, but my Venus is completely new. It is made in my own technique and is the result of my experiments with different mediums. My Venus rises in the world of contemporary art as a reminder of the achievements of artists of the past years. In Rising of Venus, the artist reimagines the birth of the goddess not from the sea, but from the cosmos itself. Venus here embodies both the celestial planet and the divine muse of beauty and creation. Her radiant golden hair — painted with liquid metallic gold that shimmers in the light — evokes the brilliance of sunlight spreading through space. Inspired by Botticelli’s iconic Birth of Venus, whose golden strands also gleam with divine luminosity, this work transforms that classical ideal into a contemporary vision. The cosmic background, created through experimental techniques and unconventional materials, merges traditional painting with modern artistic exploration. This Venus symbolizes the awakening of art itself — a new wave of creativity rising from the universe.

Twilight in a Rainy forest

The painting depicts a rainy forest at twilight in an abstract style, where the last traces of sunlight still glimmer on the tree trunks. Darkness has not fully settled, allowing the forest’s vivid, shifting colors to come alive beneath the falling rain.

Acrylic and Liquid Metal on Canvas, 2025

Secret Garden

An impressionistic painting inspired by the pansies growing in my own garden. The work captures a warm summer afternoon, when the sun begins to soften but the day is still full of light. At this time of day, the colours of the garden seem especially vivid — deep violet pansies, delicate pink flowers and fresh greens come alive against each other.
Painted in my own expressive technique, the piece focuses on the feeling of being surrounded by a flourishing summer garden: bright, fragrant and full of life. It is a simple celebration of that beautiful part of the day when you can pause and enjoy the garden before evening begins.

Oil on Canvas, 2026

Dandelions on a summer day

This acrylic painting is created using my signature bubble technique, forming soft, organic patterns that evoke a field of dandelions in bloom. Light and airy, the composition captures the fleeting moment when the seeds are ready to drift away, as if they might lift gently into the sky at any second. The piece conveys a sense of freedom, stillness, and quiet wonder, inviting the viewer to pause and breathe in its softness.
